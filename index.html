Approach

How I think
about code.

Four principles I apply on every project, small or large.

Architecture first

Schema, indexes and module boundaries are decided before the interface. They are cheaper to get right early than to change later.

Performance is a feature

I look at slow queries and page weight while building, not as a polish step at the end.

SEO is engineering

Crawlability, structured data, hreflang and canonical URLs belong in the codebase, not in a marketing checklist.

Code that outlives me

Readable, documented and plain on purpose, so the next developer can pick it up quickly.
